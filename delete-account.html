Delete your AI Mastery account

App: AI Mastery · Developer: Dev Master Mind

You can ask us to delete your AI Mastery account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to devmastermind.official@gmail.com.
  2. Send it from the email address you used to sign in (your Google account), so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. Use the subject "Delete my AI Mastery account".
  4. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address and account ID
  • Your course progress, quiz results and other app data saved with your account

What may be kept

  • Purchase and subscription records may be kept for up to 7 years where the law requires it (for example, for tax and accounting). These records are handled by Google Play and our subscription provider (RevenueCat).
  • Advertising data (such as your device's advertising ID) is processed by Google AdMob under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Note: Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Please cancel it first in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

Questions

Contact us at devmastermind.official@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
