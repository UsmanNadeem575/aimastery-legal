Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

AI MASTERY is developed by DevMasterMind. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using AI MASTERY, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Account (optional): If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. They are saved on your device to show your profile.
  • Certificate Details: When you claim a certificate, we collect the name and email address you enter, the certificate type and an anonymous app user ID, so we can create your certificate and email it to you.
  • Purchase Information: Payments are processed by Google Play. Through RevenueCat we only receive whether Pro is active. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Advertising and Device Data: For free users, our advertising partner Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address and app usage data to show and measure ads.
  • Data on Your Device: Your chosen language, learning progress and earned badges are saved only on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create your certificates and send them to your email.
  • To provide course content and Pro features.
  • To verify purchases and restore Pro access.
  • To show ads to free users.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To keep the app working well and improve it.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the services needed to run the app, and each of them handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication: optional sign-in.
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat: purchases and Pro status.
  • Google AdMob: ads for free users.
  • Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail: certificate records and certificate emails.

5. Ads and Your Choices

Free users see ads; Pro users see no ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

  • You can use the app without signing in.
  • To get a copy of your certificate data or have it deleted, email us and we will handle your request within 30 days.
  • Data saved on your device is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

8. Children’s Privacy

AI MASTERY is meant for students and adult learners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Free and Pro Access

  • Pro users: Full access to all AI, ML and DL courses, AI Automation, Prompt Engineering, projects and certificates, with no ads.
  • Free users: Access to the first lessons of each course, with ads.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available in the app, and the date at the top shows when it was last changed.
